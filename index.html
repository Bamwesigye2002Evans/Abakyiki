OUR HISTORY · JOVOC LEAGUE · CLASS OF 2023

FROM ST JOSEPH'S TO ABAKIKI

Founded in 2023, Abakiki FC carried its school roots into the JOVOC League with a vision to play fearless, fluid football and represent our community with pride.

From early training sessions to a shared dream of lifting the league trophy, the Class of 2023 is a brotherhood built on growth, grit, and the beautiful game.

OUR STORY · 2017-2023

From St Joseph's to Abakiki

The Abakiki story grew from school roots into a shared football ambition, finding its next chapter in the JOVOC League and the Class of 2023.

2017-2019

St Joseph's School

St Joseph's is where this story begins. School life brought people together, building friendships, discipline, and a sense of community that would become the foundation for a shared love of football.

2020-2022

A wider football ambition

The connections formed at school carried beyond the classroom. A growing ambition to play, compete, and represent the community set the stage for a club identity and a place in organized league football.

2023

Abakiki and the JOVOC League

Abakiki FC became part of the JOVOC League's inaugural Class of 2023. The team carried its school roots into a new chapter, representing its community with pride and building a future together on and off the pitch.
